## About Our Web App
We're making easier to learn piano notes for aspiring musicians! Our mission is straightforward: offer a free, open-source platform without subscriptions so that anyone can learn and enjoy playing the piano.

### Frustration with Subscription Models
We understand the frustration of existing apps requiring subscriptions to access their features. That's why our app is completely subscription-free, ensuring music remains accessible to all without any financial barriers.

### Our Vision: A Community-Driven Experience
Piano Note isn't just about learning; it's a community effort! Here are some key aspects:

 - **Free and Open:** We believe in open-source software, making our app entirely free and transparent. No hidden costs or subscriptions needed!

 - **Privacy First:** Your privacy is respected. We don't collect personal data, ensuring a safe practice environment without tracking or interruptions.

 - **Community Building:** Together with other pianists and music enthusiasts, we aim to create an inclusive community where sharing progress, collaborating, and fostering creativity are the norm. 

Join us on this musical journey as we make piano learning accessible, fun, and subscription-free! Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve our app for a better user experience. Let's play some tunes together! 🎹
