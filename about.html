About Piano Notes

We're making easier to learn piano notes for aspiring musicians. Our mission is simple: provide a free, no-subscription, and open-source platform for aspiring pianists.

Frustration with Subscription Models

We understand the frustration that comes with most existing apps in this domain requiring subscriptions. We believe music should be accessible to everyone without any financial barriers. That's why we've created an app where you can practice piano scores without paying a single penny.

Our Vision

  • Free and Open: Our web app is completely free to use, no hidden costs or subscriptions. We believe in the power of open-source software, ensuring our code is transparent and available for everyone.
  • Privacy First: Rest assured that we do not collect any personal data from our users. Your privacy is paramount, so you can practice your piano scores without worrying about data tracking or ads interrupting your journey.
  • Community Driven: We're building a community of passionate pianists and music enthusiasts. Share your progress, collaborate with others, and contribute to an environment that fosters growth and creativity.

Join us on this musical journey as we embark on making piano practice accessible, fun, and subscription-free! Your feedback is always welcome and helps shape our app for the better. Let's play some tunes together!
